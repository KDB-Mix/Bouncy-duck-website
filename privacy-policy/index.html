Privacy Policy for Bouncy Duck

Effective date: August 4, 2026
Developer: kmstudios.dev
Contact: support@kmstudios.dev

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Bouncy Duck (the “Game”). It explains how the developer handles information in connection with the Game.

1) Summary

  • No ads: The Game does not display advertisements.
  • No external SDKs: The developer does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • No gameplay data collection: The Game does not collect personal data for gameplay.
  • Offline by design: The Game does not connect to a server for gameplay, scoring, or progress.
  • Progress on device only: Game progress is stored locally on your device.

2) Information the Game collects

The Game does not collect personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, or any device identifiers for gameplay.

The Game is designed to operate without internet connectivity and does not perform network requests for gameplay or progress.

3) Local progress

Progress (such as scores and related offline gameplay information) is stored only on your device. The Game does not provide cloud saving.

4) Crash reporting and platform safety mechanisms

Apps distributed through application stores (such as the Google Play Store) may receive standard platform-provided crash diagnostics and safety/quality signals. These mechanisms are provided by the store and/or the platform environment.

The developer does not integrate Sentry or other third-party crash reporting SDKs and does not add additional crash reporting beyond what is provided by the platform/store environment.

5) Permissions

The Game does not request permissions (beyond standard requirements for installation and runtime) and does not access sensitive data such as location, contacts, microphone, or camera.

6) Children / all ages

The Game is intended for all ages, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7) Data sharing

Because the Game does not collect personal data for gameplay and does not use external tracking/advertising SDKs, there is no sharing of personal data with third parties in connection with the Game.

8) International users

This Privacy Policy applies worldwide. If local laws impose additional requirements, the developer will comply with those requirements as applicable.

9) Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer: support@kmstudios.dev.
